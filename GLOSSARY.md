# Anime365 viewing

Terms used when finding and watching Anime365 episodes in IINA.

## Language

**Anime365**:
The anime streaming service available at smotret-anime.org.
_Avoid_: treating Anime365 and smotret-anime.org as different services

**Series**:
An Anime365 catalog entry that groups episodes.
_Avoid_: show, title when referring to the catalog entry rather than its name

**Episode**:
An installment within a series, with its own available translations.

**Translation**:
An Anime365 version of an episode distinguished by its language, audio or subtitle type, and credited authors.
_Avoid_: using translation to mean playback resolution

**Viewing history**:
A record of episodes a viewer has played and the playback position reached in each episode.

**Continue watching**:
A per-series selection for returning to an unfinished episode at its saved position, or starting the next episode after the previous one finishes.

**Translation preference**:
A viewer's remembered choice of translation characteristics for a series. Each episode has its own translations, so a preference can match different translations across episodes.
_Avoid_: treating a translation ID as a choice shared by every episode in a series

**Autoplay**:
Automatic playback of the next episode after the current episode finishes.

**Completed episode**:
An episode whose playback has reached its end. Stopping during the credits does not by itself make an episode completed.

**Playback resolution**:
The video dimensions of a playback option for a translation.
_Avoid_: translation source classifications such as TV or Blu-ray
